# Independent V2 review — geometry, performance character and production safety

**Date:** 2026-10-11  
**Reviewer:** design/technical review following explicit owner critique  
**Input:** `render-concept.mjs`, `ghost-aero-*.png`, `RENDER_REVIEW.md`, M3P factory dimensions and production identity references.  
**Status:** V2 archived as exploration. V3 needed.

## Owner feedback (confirmed)

1. Multiple long, narrow, largely parallel streamlines evoke **luxury and calm GT design**, not energetic/track-oriented performance.
2. The current full side silhouette is inaccurate; it must be reconstructed from **genuine M3P geometry**, not embellished guessed Bézier curves.
3. Use an **online-sourced Tesla T SVG** instead of drawing a logo approximation.

## Aesthetic findings

**A1 / Low visual tension (severe).** 28 paths are distributed across upper shoulder/lower side/fender/rear with repeated smooth, near-horizontal trajectories. The visual gradient is dominated by line *count* rather than sharp acceleration cues, interruption, directional collision or fragmentation. Even at night it reads decorative.

**A2 / Lack of hierarchy (high).** Many long paths have similar length/weight and run in the same direction. The rear haunch, not the calm central door, must host the focal *event*. Avoid both a regular luxury pinstripe and a crude large race stripe.

**A3 / T motifs lose identity (high).** The 78 small provisional path icons appear as noise at ordinary viewing distance. Density varies but the shapes lack an explicit strand-to-glyph conversion; the resulting patch resembles tiny random specks.

**A4 / MODEL 2.9 identity is understated to the point of being generic (medium).** Low-contrast text near lower door does not create the look of a credible experiment package. Replace generic "engineering-looking" label stacks with few purposeful datum/section callouts anchored to a feature and a visibly hierarchical prototype serial.

**A5 / Presence of a fictitious vehicle harms the result (severe).** Large body drawing resembles a generic sedan rather than the 2024+ M3P. We should not judge graphic design on top of an inaccurate substrate.

## Technical findings (confirmed by inspecting source)

**T1 / Body shell is a freehand SVG path.** `const body = "M 121 605 ..."` and separate hard-coded `windows`, `seams`, `handles`, `wheel()` and detail paths approximate a sedan. A side-profile dimension ratio alone cannot validate roof contour, hood taper, window cut, C-pillar, wheel-arch openings, rear lip/spoiler or Performance bumper/sill features.

**T2 / Shape-dependent flow is NOT implemented.** `S1` and `S2` are almost parallel lines made from fixed-coordinate sine/polynomial offsets. `R1` rear strokes likewise lack a normal/tangent reference to measured ridge geometry. The earlier claim "ridge aligned" was a design intent, not a verified computation.

**T3 / Tesla T not sourced.** `tMaster` is a two-piece hand-coded approximation, not the precise silhouette.

**T4 / Placement/density is screen-space only.** Particle x/y, Gaussian peak and rejection radius are hard-coded in schematic pixels; generated sample size has no mm scale or mapping for UV seams and material weeding. The proposed Poisson/flow-tangent coupling was not fully implemented.

**T5 / Night is visual RGB contrast.** Color palettes differ day/night; `softGlow` blur isn't a model of retroreflection. Device/headlamp geometry, material gloss and PPF compatibility remain untested.

**T6 / Reproducibility good but multi-view accuracy incomplete.** SVG/PNG day/night do come from the same scene, which is a real improvement over V1, but the vehicle shell is not 3D-registered. Current render uses one side only, no hood/rear panel test.

**T7 / Reference photo rights.** Tesla's public Media Gallery terms restrict redistribution and non-editorial uses. Use it to find shapes or private research as appropriate; do not re-host product photography in a public repo without permission. Prefer user-provided aligned images/allowed source for any public geometry layer.

## Geometry ground truth

Tesla's 2024+ M3P manual lists approximate: **length 4724 mm, height 1430 mm, wheelbase 2875 mm, front overhang 872 mm, rear overhang 977 mm**. Dimension scaling anchors gross layout but **cannot produce an OEM contour**; fit a perpendicular image and/or verified 3D geometry.

## Decision

Stop iterating V2's 28-strand layout directly. Treat it as archived stylistic baseline. V3's first mandatory gate is M3P silhouette registration; new livery must pass a **no-lines vs 2–4 highly-directed energy events** comparison with significantly more coherent negative space.

## Open uncertainties

- Real vehicle photos from near-orthographic side and two 3/4 angles (or a licensed accurate M3P mesh).
- Exact rear spoiler, wrap/PPF edges and any owner's aftermarket aero hardware *as-installed*.
- Adhesive, cut/weed, wash and optical test results for selected matte-black and black reflective materials.
- Canonical shape approval vs Simple Icons normalized Tesla T reference, especially tiny cut-outs.
