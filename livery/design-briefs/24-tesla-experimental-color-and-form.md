## The exactly-three visual changes for #24
1. **Organic load-path rather than armor slashes**: one sculpturally muscular primary path tied to real rear quarter/arch structural geography, with thickness compression / swelling / deliberate termination. Remove unnecessary forward fender and lower-door cuts, recover black front-door space. Not a GT long soft parallel pinstripe either.
2. **Couple genuine aero edges + micro Tesla T field**: use the rear spoiler and diffuser as visual end points, not a pasted collection of blade motifs; T elements distributed with discrete count/spacing changes and nonrectangular boundary. Actual owner's installed wing/diffuser contours must not be guessed from uninspected photographs.
3. **Functional color coding only**: proposal C red/titanium/black, red 1–2 engineering landmarks instead of decorative slashes, titanium for very limited volumetric emphasis. NIGHT every reflective part shows **same uniform brightness and color with absolutely no reflectance gradients**. Non-reflective red stays its material color, not magically white. Preserve DAY black stealth.
