# V2 prototype 01 — visual/engineering QA log

**Generated:** 2026-10-11  
**Reviewed assets:** `ghost-aero-day.png`, `ghost-aero-night.png`, `ghost-aero-anatomy.png`, `ghost-aero-detail.png`  
**Status:** First concept evidence; **not owner-approved**.

## What works

- One **identical scene** drives day/night visuals, unlike the disconnected V1 generators.
- A shoulder-guided curve family, lower sculptural echo and a localized rear haunch fan now visually describe a single design.
- Tesla T-inspired marks have irregular positions and variable scale. The emphasis increases in the rear-door/rear-quarter area, without a full checkerboard grid.
- The `MODEL 2.9` identifier is restrained and no longer surrounded by large, decorative engineering grid lines.
- The day art reads as near-black; selected `REVEAL` entities are substantially more visible in the simulated night palette.

## Detected limitations and next fixes

1. **Geometry calibration P0:** the car outline and ridge curves are *schematic*. Do not assert they are exact M3P body creases or 3D-registered positions. Capture target car or suitable licensed full-side reference and align verified landmarks. Opposite side, hood and tail design still need to be authored.
2. **Motif fidelity P0:** the 78 symbols use a **provisional T-inspired shape**, not a verified Tesla T vector. Replace with approved canonical geometry before final design sign-off.
3. **Visibility P1:** a number of T glyphs are too fine to resolve at a reduced/full-vehicle preview. Judge high-resolution detail and **physical sample print/cut sizes** before keeping them; if needed change size distribution and strand-to-particle transition.
4. **Flow complexity P1:** some upper strands still look like parallel decorative lines rather than a clearly divergent field. Consider fewer primary paths, stronger deliberate branching, and local taper after the rear door.
5. **Material evidence P0:** all night/reflective contrast is a lighting **illustration**, not real 3M/ORALITE reflectivity. Test samples under sunlight and angled headlights against the existing matte PPF.
6. **No manufacturing source P0:** no valid cutter vectors or physical scale, no proof of adhesive compatibility, no sensor/handle clearance map, no left/right mirrored-but-readable mark QA.
7. **Presentation:** enlarged rear-quarter preview is provided to evaluate motif spacing without relying on miniature whole-car render; no overlay on real photo has been performed yet.

## Recommendation for upcoming review

Keep the broad three-layer hierarchy. Ask owner to review *specifically* whether the rear T field needs to be denser or sparser, whether the shoulder lines need fewer/thicker strands, and whether `MODEL 2.9` should stay by the lower door or migrate to another panel.

**Do not record any of these as design decisions without owner sign-off.**
