# Panel-by-Panel Composition — MODEL 2.9 / Iteration 0002

**Status: placement hypotheses**, to be approved after a real vehicle side/three-quarter/plan view and Tesla 3D Paint Shop checks.

| Region | Aero Flow | Tesla T Parametric Fade | Blueprint / identity | Priority |

### Guiding composition, not fixed physical geometry

**Proposed lateral rhythm:** origin at front wheel → long negative-space-led forward-door flow → rear-door branching → **rear haunch T field** → clean disappearance near the tail.

Do **not** treat the outer edge of a white region in Tesla's UV mask as a physical panel outline or assume a 90°/180° flip. Confirm winding and real panel labels in vehicle preview.

## P01. Hood

**Art direction:** stillness, precision, aerodynamic volume. Limit to two or three families of fine longitudinal strokes (which may branch) aligned with verified hood creases/highlights, with most of the hood left clean. Avoid a symmetrical "racing stripe" composition.

**Blueprint:** a single `MODEL 2.9` descriptor or short calibrated arc can sit toward the rear section if the full-size mockup justifies it. Do not visually compete with the Tesla hood emblem.

**No-go:** covering bonnet badge, camera/safety areas, large central chevrons, overly obvious eye-shaped loops. Check hood curvature in 3/4.

## P02. Front fender and front wheel arch

**Art direction:** controlled birth of the side graphic. A compact bundle begins in the wake/shape of the wheel arch and changes tangent toward the upper-side body contour; no sudden thick stripes.

**Tesla T:** ultra-sparse micro-motifs at the transition from wing to front door, not a big cluster.

**Blueprint:** if chosen, place `EVT-2.9-0001` adjacent to a micro-datum / tick (avoid the camera/fender repeater hardware).

**No-go:** badge/repeater occlusion, wrapping thin decals around highly stretched wheel arch edges, fake precision numbers.

## P03. Front door

**Art direction:** *breathing room*. Carry only a selected number of main Aero strands across the upper shoulder-volume band and/or lower sculpted door section. Let the OEM sheetmetal read; this is the long calm section that makes the rear flare feel earned.

**Tesla T:** isolated transitional particles if needed; do not build a repetitive mini-logo wallpaper.

**Blueprint:** usually empty. Small signage only if the vehicle needs a balance correction.

**No-go:** a complete fill of parallel vertical lines, prominent stripe across door handle, heavy patterns near rocker stone-chip zone.

## P04. Rear door

**Art direction:** begin the meaningful transition — some strokes compress, some separate, some terminate into particle groups. Let curvature and local flow control the effect, not a global x/y density ramp.

**Tesla T:** density increases asymmetrically within the design zone while retaining deliberate negative pockets. Rotation follows local flow direction, preserving Tesla T geometry.

**Blueprint:** optional datum that identifies the start of "flow-field" release, far subordinate to main livery.

**No-go:** an abrupt seam of graphics where the door meets the rear quarter, hard rectangular checkerboard edge.

## P05. Rear quarter / rear haunch (visual climax)

**Art direction:** the most memorable area. Primary strands climb or peel around the visually confirmed rear shoulder/haunch shape; some become micro Tesla T motifs. One localized particle crescendo occurs **in front of / around the haunch volume**, then dissolves before the tail lamp boundary.

**Placement safeguards:** keep the wheel arch perimeter and rear lamp/body gap largely clear; avoid a dense field that looks like a block from distance. Size distribution must be tolerant to cutting.

**Blueprint:** a tiny secondary test ID may sit in a quiet pocket, **not** layered over the densest Tesla T field. Show T marks as *textures*, not many fully readable brand billboards.

**No-go:** particle material saturating an entire rear fender, random dots without directional continuity, line endings that collide with light assemblies.

## P06. Rear bumper, trunk and tail

**Art direction:** stop, do not start a new theme. Very few short echo-lines or particles if the body profile benefits; otherwise entirely blank to protect the clean tail geometry and rear wing emphasis.

**Safety:** rear-facing white/silver retroreflective designs require local regulatory review; do not cover/compromise lamps, plate, reflectors, or cameras. Material layout can be a NO-PRINT mask.

## P07. Left/right consistency

Both sides share **semantic structure and similar visual mass**, not necessarily pixel-mirrored paths. A forward-facing arrow should point appropriately on both sides; logos and text remain normal reading direction. Treat seam locations and curvature separately, and inspect both sides in Tesla Paint Shop or a verified 3D mockup.

## Three-level focal map

1. **Primary:** rear door → rear quarter transition (Aero → Tesla T).
2. **Secondary:** front fender entry point and selective long door flow.
3. **Tertiary:** `MODEL 2.9` technical signature / hood reference annotations.

Keep the roof/window glass and majority of outer skin silent. The design must still look good with **all reflectivity turned off**.

## Inspection gates, panel by panel

For each panel, capture: front three-quarter, rear three-quarter, left/right side and a closer seam view; flag line direction, projection distortion, 3D motif orientation, overprinting and exposed sticker-edge density. Review under ambient daytime, glare daytime and actual point-source night lighting. Paint Shop can check mapping, **not optical reflectivity or cut quality**.

## Physical constraints (not absolute dimensions)

Pending installer test, start a physical sample card containing ~3/4/5/6 mm *candidate* narrow strips and ~15/20/30/40 mm glyph variants. Treat values as **test specimens only**, not safe production minima. Record successful weed/adhesion/wash/removal behavior before finalizing manufacturing rules. No direct cutting on existing PPF.
