# Materials: candidate study (not purchasing approval)

| Family | Daytime finish | Night effect | Current status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Black retroreflective automotive vinyl | Black, often glossier than matte PPF | Reflects incoming headlights / flash | **Candidate** |
| Clear UV-reactive film | Potentially low daytime visibility; inspect transparency/haze | Fluoresces under UV, not ordinary headlight reflection | **Candidate** |
| Clear/milky photoluminescent vinyl | Usually visible white/green haze | Glows after charging then fades | Lower preference given stealth daytime priority |
| Standard matte-black vinyl | Matte stealth | Little additional reflection | Baseline/control candidate |

Previously discussed black retroreflective options include **3M Scotchlite 680CR-85** and **ORALITE 5600RA Black**. Their behavior on Apterix matte PPF and removability **have not been verified**.

Before installation: request material samples, photograph in natural daylight and with headlights from realistic angles, verify glossy-vs-matte difference, contact existing PPF supplier and installer, inspect removal behavior. Never infer physical brightness from rendered screenshots. Do not place white-reflective material where vehicle regulations restrict reflector colors.
